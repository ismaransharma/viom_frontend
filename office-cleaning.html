What's Included

Every area, covered.

Workstations

  • Wipe all desks and work surfaces
  • Dust monitors and equipment (exterior)
  • Clean keyboards and phones (exterior)
  • Empty and reline desk bins

Common Areas

  • Clean reception desk and surfaces
  • Wipe meeting room tables and chairs
  • Wipe whiteboards (if instructed)
  • Dust shelving and cabinets

Bathrooms

  • Scrub and disinfect toilets
  • Clean sinks, tapware, and mirrors
  • Mop floor and wipe tiles
  • Restock soap and paper (consumables extra)

Kitchen / Breakroom

  • Wipe benchtops and splashback
  • Clean sink and tapware
  • Wipe microwave and fridge (exterior)
  • Empty bins and mop floor

Floors & Entrance

  • Vacuum carpeted areas
  • Mop hard floors and hallways
  • Clean entrance glass doors
  • Dust light fittings in common areas

Regular or one-off?

We offer daily, weekly, and fortnightly office cleans. One-off cleans also available for events or move-out situations.
