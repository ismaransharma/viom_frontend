House Cleaning

A Consistent Clean,
Every Visit.

We clean all living areas, bedrooms, bathrooms, and kitchens. Whether you need a regular weekly clean or a one-off visit, our team follows the same checklist every time.

What's Included

Every room, every time.

Living Areas

  • Dust all surfaces and shelving
  • Vacuum upholstered furniture
  • Vacuum and mop floors
  • Clean mirrors and glass
  • Wipe skirting boards

Bedrooms

  • Make beds and straighten linen
  • Dust furniture and surfaces
  • Vacuum floors and rugs
  • Wipe windowsills
  • Empty and reline bins

Bathrooms

  • Scrub and disinfect toilet
  • Clean sink and tapware
  • Scrub shower and/or bath
  • Wipe mirrors and surfaces
  • Mop floor

Kitchen

  • Wipe all benchtops
  • Clean stovetop and drip trays
  • Wipe appliance exteriors
  • Clean sink and tapware
  • Mop floor

Throughout

  • Empty and reline all bins
  • Wipe light switches and door handles
  • Dust ceiling fans and light fittings
  • Wipe internal window glass
